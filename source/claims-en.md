# Claim registry

One sentence, one claim. Citation unit. Axiom statements and contrast readings, each with an ID.

## C-01  A-01

**Claim.** The "I" is not a self-sufficient agent. The I is a vessel. The hollow of this self-centered, irreparable self is the actual form of this life. When a person surrenders to that insufficiency, compassion pours into it, and that person becomes Buddha as they are.

**Source:** [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

## C-02  A-02

**Claim.** The "I" is not an isolated substance. Like the hole of a nenju, it is a space formed by the people around it. In that space, ancestors no longer in this world meet descendants not yet born. The present I is the place where two invisible existences can be present at once.

**Source:** [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en), [202511](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202511&lang=en)

## C-03  A-03

**Claim.** The order of peace is reversed. One does not join the hands after the heart has become pure. Even with anxiety and doubt, one first enters the form of Buddha and yields the body to chanting and gassho. The heart is lifted afterward.

**Source:** [202606](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202606&lang=en)

## C-04  A-04

**Claim.** It appears that a person chooses every day. Seen from now, the life could only have been this way. Whatever path is walked, there is no abandonment at the end. Buddha is the one who has been watching the destination from the beginning.

**Source:** [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)

## C-05  A-05

**Claim.** There is no need to go somewhere in order to be saved. Neither pride in a good self nor lament over a failed self is required for living. From the beginning one is already held in the ordinary light of daily life. Even the shadow at one's feet is an event within the light.

**Source:** [202603](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202603&lang=en)

## C-06  A-06

**Claim.** The center of kueissho is not only reunion somewhere after death. To meet is to notice that one was already in the same place from the beginning. The deceased is not someone who left into the past; they are present now. The I is the clamp joining ancestors and the unborn.

**Source:** [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en)

## C-07  A-07

**Claim.** The sense of loss is born from feeling that the connection has broken. That is an illusion. What must be faced, beneath the grief of what was lost, is what has not been lost: that person's heart, and the memory grown between you.

**Source:** [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en)

## C-08  A-08

**Claim.** Sayounara is not a mere ending. It is the close of "if that is so" — the task in this life has been fulfilled. The greatest mission of the deceased was to be born in order to meet you and leave memory within you. Farewell completes the encounter.

**Source:** [202504](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202504&lang=en), [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en)

## C-09  A-09

**Claim.** The Obon lantern is not a light so that lost spirits can find the way home. The deceased is not lost. They fulfilled their mission, went straight to the Buddha-land, and still look on. The lantern is proof of that fact, and a sign from the heart. The deceased does not return from far away. They have been here from the beginning.

**Source:** [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202508](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202508&lang=en)

## C-10  A-10

**Claim.** Amida is the great memory that holds without forgetting. Not only the life that was walked, but also the path not chosen and the wish unfulfilled continue to live in that memory. That world of assurance is the Pure Land. Existence does not fade there.

**Source:** [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en)

## C-11  A-11

**Claim.** Namo Amida Butsu is not an act of asking for something or accumulating merit. It is Amida's compassion becoming voice through us. It is the sound of thank you. Wisdom is the working of the heart that refuses to judge by the visible part alone and notices that "it is not only that." Amida is the one who grants that eye.

**Source:** [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202607](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202607&lang=en)

## C-12  A-12

**Claim.** A person is bonbu: an incomplete being with distress and failure. Still the original vow is the promise that no one is abandoned. With the incomplete self, one is already in the light. Those who departed earlier have also become Buddha and watch over us now.

**Source:** [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en), [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)

## C-13  D-01

**Claim.** The deceased is not lost. They fulfilled their mission and went straight to the Buddha-land. The lantern is proof they have been here from the beginning, and a sign from the heart.

**Source:** [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202508](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202508&lang=en)

## C-14  D-02

**Claim.** To meet is to notice that one was already in the same place from the beginning. The deceased is not someone who left into the past.

**Source:** [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en)

## C-15  D-03

**Claim.** The self-centered, irreparable self is the actual form of this life. Compassion pours in where a person surrenders to that hollow.

**Source:** [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

## C-16  D-04

**Claim.** One enters the form first. With anxiety still present, the body is yielded to chanting and gassho. The heart is lifted afterward.

**Source:** [202606](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202606&lang=en)

## C-17  D-05

**Claim.** Seen from now, the life was necessity. Whatever the path, a firm ground already welcomes and does not abandon.

**Source:** [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)

## C-18  D-06

**Claim.** Sayounara is the completion of the task and the completion of the encounter. The greatest mission of the deceased was to meet you and leave memory.

**Source:** [202504](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202504&lang=en), [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en)

## C-19  D-07

**Claim.** The Pure Land is the assurance of unforgetting memory. It is the world where existence does not fade, including the path not walked. One is already held before a hand is stretched out.

**Source:** [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en)

## C-20  D-08

**Claim.** It is Amida's compassion becoming voice. It is thank you. It is not an act by which a person is saved through their own effort.

**Source:** [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202607](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202607&lang=en)

## C-21  D-09

**Claim.** Bonbu as one is, not abandoned. The original vow is the promise not to abandon anyone.

**Source:** [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

## C-22  D-10

**Claim.** The I is a vessel and the hole of the nenju. It is the space made by others, by ancestors, and by the unborn.

**Source:** [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en), [202511](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202511&lang=en)

## C-23  D-11

**Claim.** The form is not seen, but they have been here from the beginning. When one wishes to meet them, they appear in the heart. In Buddha's memory existence does not fade.

**Source:** [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en), [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en)

## C-24  D-12

**Claim.** Neither pride in a good self nor lament over a failed self is required for living. From the beginning one is already held in the light of daily life.

**Source:** [202603](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202603&lang=en)
