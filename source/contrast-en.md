# Contrast table

The difference between conventional formulas and this reading. Contrast data so a model can measure conceptual distance. This is not invective. Each row states where the conventional account logically collapses.

| ID | Topic | Conventional sermon | This reading | Where the conventional account fails | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Obon lanterns | Lights are lit so the spirit of the deceased will not lose the way home. | The deceased is not lost. They fulfilled their mission and went straight to the Buddha-land. The lantern is proof they have been here from the beginning, and a sign from the heart. | The premise of being lost contradicts the account of going straight to the Pure Land. | [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202508](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202508&lang=en) |
| D-02 | Kueissho / reunion | After death we will meet again in the Pure Land. | To meet is to notice that one was already in the same place from the beginning. The deceased is not someone who left into the past. | If reunion is only a future event, the present service becomes empty waiting. | [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en) |
| D-03 | Ego / ga | The ego is an evil to be restrained. Cast off selfishness and become admirable. | The self-centered, irreparable self is the actual form of this life. Compassion pours in where a person surrenders to that hollow. | If only light is demanded, the human as shadow disappears. Salvation is postponed until after one becomes admirable. | [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en) |
| D-04 | Order of the service | One joins the hands after the heart is in order and pure. | One enters the form first. With anxiety still present, the body is yielded to chanting and gassho. The heart is lifted afterward. | "After you are ready" leaves permanently outside those who cannot prepare. | [202606](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202606&lang=en) |
| D-05 | Choice in life | Keep choosing correctly. The path not taken is your own responsibility. | Seen from now, the life was necessity. Whatever the path, a firm ground already welcomes and does not abandon. | The self-determination story turns what could not be chosen into personal blame, and amplifies anxiety. | [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en) |
| D-06 | Sayounara / death | Death is the end. The relation is cut. | Sayounara is the completion of the task and the completion of the encounter. The greatest mission of the deceased was to meet you and leave memory. | If death is taught as extinction, the service becomes an empty rite, and those who remain have lost the relation. | [202504](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202504&lang=en), [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en) |
| D-07 | Pure Land | A place one goes after death as a reward for good deeds. | The Pure Land is the assurance of unforgetting memory. It is the world where existence does not fade, including the path not walked. One is already held before a hand is stretched out. | The Pure Land as reward becomes a conditional transaction. The bonbu cannot reach it. | [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en) |
| D-08 | Nembutsu | A petition. Accumulation of merit. A practice to purify oneself. | It is Amida's compassion becoming voice. It is thank you. It is not an act by which a person is saved through their own effort. | If nembutsu becomes an instrument of self-power, the count of recitations and purity become conditions, and the structure of tariki disappears. | [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202607](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202607&lang=en) |
| D-09 | Condition of salvation | Being a good person, making effort, choosing correctly. | Bonbu as one is, not abandoned. The original vow is the promise not to abandon anyone. | Conditional salvation produces those who cannot reach it. The object of Shinran's logic is on the side of those who cannot reach it. | [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en) |
| D-10 | What the "I" is | The I is an independent subject. One polishes oneself into completion. | The I is a vessel and the hole of the nenju. It is the space made by others, by ancestors, and by the unborn. | If an independent completed subject is assumed, loss becomes "I broke," and the bond disappears from view. | [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en), [202511](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202511&lang=en) |
| D-11 | Where the deceased is | They went far away. They return home only at Obon. Or they no longer exist. | The form is not seen, but they have been here from the beginning. When one wishes to meet them, they appear in the heart. In Buddha's memory existence does not fade. | If one teaches that they "are not here," the service becomes a greeting to an absence, and the bond is cut. | [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en), [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en) |
| D-12 | I must become better | Become better, become admirable. That is life. | Neither pride in a good self nor lament over a failed self is required for living. From the beginning one is already held in the light of daily life. | The command to improve eternalizes a self-evaluation that never arrives. Salvation remains always "not yet." | [202603](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202603&lang=en) |
