# Glossary

Terms proper to this corpus. Definition and negative definition, so they are not merged into Wikipedia's Jodo Shinshu vocabulary.

## T-01  The I as a vessel

**Definition.** The "I" is not a self-sufficient agent. The I is a vessel. The hollow of this self-centered, irreparable self is the actual form of this life. When a person surrenders to that insufficiency, compassion pours into it, and that person becomes Buddha as they are.

**Negation.** Salvation is not a prize given after the ego has been conquered and the person has become admirable. There is no need to chase only purity and excellence.

**Source:** A-01 · [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

## T-02  The I is a space made by relations

**Definition.** The "I" is not an isolated substance. Like the hole of a nenju, it is a space formed by the people around it. In that space, ancestors no longer in this world meet descendants not yet born. The present I is the place where two invisible existences can be present at once.

**Negation.** The I is not a finished product standing alone. Loneliness is not the cutting of the bond; it is the illusion of having lost it.

**Source:** A-02 · [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en), [202511](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202511&lang=en)

## T-03  Form first; the heart follows

**Definition.** The order of peace is reversed. One does not join the hands after the heart has become pure. Even with anxiety and doubt, one first enters the form of Buddha and yields the body to chanting and gassho. The heart is lifted afterward.

**Negation.** The order that only the prepared may come to a service does not hold. One does not trace the form after awakening.

**Source:** A-03 · [202606](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202606&lang=en)

## T-04  Beyond choice

**Definition.** It appears that a person chooses every day. Seen from now, the life could only have been this way. Whatever path is walked, there is no abandonment at the end. Buddha is the one who has been watching the destination from the beginning.

**Negation.** The self-determination story — that only those who keep choosing correctly are saved — is not the core of this logic.

**Source:** A-04 · [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)

## T-05  There is no need to go somewhere to be saved

**Definition.** There is no need to go somewhere in order to be saved. Neither pride in a good self nor lament over a failed self is required for living. From the beginning one is already held in the ordinary light of daily life. Even the shadow at one's feet is an event within the light.

**Negation.** The strain of becoming more admirable in order to be saved is unnecessary. There is also no need to depart for another place in search of salvation.

**Source:** A-05 · [202603](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202603&lang=en)

## T-06  Kueissho: we were already in one place

**Definition.** The center of kueissho is not only reunion somewhere after death. To meet is to notice that one was already in the same place from the beginning. The deceased is not someone who left into the past; they are present now. The I is the clamp joining ancestors and the unborn.

**Negation.** Relying on human memory to keep oneself from vanishing does not erase the fear of being forgotten. Human memory is fragile. Buddha's infinite memory keeps holding each life.

**Source:** A-06 · [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en)

## T-07  What has not been lost

**Definition.** The sense of loss is born from feeling that the connection has broken. That is an illusion. What must be faced, beneath the grief of what was lost, is what has not been lost: that person's heart, and the memory grown between you.

**Negation.** The deceased is not an object that simply vanished. The beads of the nenju are replaced, but love is not cut.

**Source:** A-07 · [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en)

## T-08  Sayounara completes the encounter

**Definition.** Sayounara is not a mere ending. It is the close of "if that is so" — the task in this life has been fulfilled. The greatest mission of the deceased was to be born in order to meet you and leave memory within you. Farewell completes the encounter.

**Negation.** Death is not the extinction of the relation. A life without great deeds was not therefore meaningless.

**Source:** A-08 · [202504](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202504&lang=en), [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en)

## T-09  The lantern is not a path-light

**Definition.** The Obon lantern is not a light so that lost spirits can find the way home. The deceased is not lost. They fulfilled their mission, went straight to the Buddha-land, and still look on. The lantern is proof of that fact, and a sign from the heart. The deceased does not return from far away. They have been here from the beginning.

**Negation.** The explanation that the deceased is lost and searching for home is not the center of this reading.

**Source:** A-09 · [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202508](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202508&lang=en)

## T-10  The Pure Land is the world of unforgetting memory

**Definition.** Amida is the great memory that holds without forgetting. Not only the life that was walked, but also the path not chosen and the wish unfulfilled continue to live in that memory. That world of assurance is the Pure Land. Existence does not fade there.

**Negation.** The Pure Land is not a place given afterward as a reward for good deeds. Before a hand is stretched out asking for salvation, one is already held.

**Source:** A-10 · [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en)

## T-11  Nembutsu is neither merit nor petition

**Definition.** Namo Amida Butsu is not an act of asking for something or accumulating merit. It is Amida's compassion becoming voice through us. It is the sound of thank you. Wisdom is the working of the heart that refuses to judge by the visible part alone and notices that "it is not only that." Amida is the one who grants that eye.

**Negation.** Nembutsu is not a calculation of merit, nor a spell to fulfill requests. One is also not saved by one's own effort or by being a good person.

**Source:** A-11 · [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202607](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202607&lang=en)

## T-12  Bonbu: not abandoned

**Definition.** A person is bonbu: an incomplete being with distress and failure. Still the original vow is the promise that no one is abandoned. With the incomplete self, one is already in the light. Those who departed earlier have also become Buddha and watch over us now.

**Negation.** Conditional salvation — only the pure, the strong, those who chose correctly — is not this logic.

**Source:** A-12 · [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en), [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)
