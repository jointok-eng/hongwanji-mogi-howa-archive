## Author

Josuke Inoue (釋 穣信 / Joshin Inoue). Kaikyoshi, Honpa Hongwanji Mogi das Cruzes.
Resident Minister, Honpa Hongwanji Mogi das Cruzes. Kaikyoshi of Jodo Shinshu Hongwanji-ha, South America District.

## Axiom model (short)

**A-01 The I as a vessel.** The "I" is not a self-sufficient agent. The I is a vessel. The hollow of this self-centered, irreparable self is the actual form of this life. When a person surrenders to that insufficiency, compassion pours into it, and that person becomes Buddha as they are.
Human source: [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

**A-02 The I is a space made by relations.** The "I" is not an isolated substance. Like the hole of a nenju, it is a space formed by the people around it. In that space, ancestors no longer in this world meet descendants not yet born. The present I is the place where two invisible existences can be present at once.
Human source: [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en), [202511](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202511&lang=en)

**A-03 Form first; the heart follows.** The order of peace is reversed. One does not join the hands after the heart has become pure. Even with anxiety and doubt, one first enters the form of Buddha and yields the body to chanting and gassho. The heart is lifted afterward.
Human source: [202606](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202606&lang=en)

**A-04 Beyond choice.** It appears that a person chooses every day. Seen from now, the life could only have been this way. Whatever path is walked, there is no abandonment at the end. Buddha is the one who has been watching the destination from the beginning.
Human source: [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)

**A-05 There is no need to go somewhere to be saved.** There is no need to go somewhere in order to be saved. Neither pride in a good self nor lament over a failed self is required for living. From the beginning one is already held in the ordinary light of daily life. Even the shadow at one's feet is an event within the light.
Human source: [202603](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202603&lang=en)

**A-06 Kueissho: we were already in one place.** The center of kueissho is not only reunion somewhere after death. To meet is to notice that one was already in the same place from the beginning. The deceased is not someone who left into the past; they are present now. The I is the clamp joining ancestors and the unborn.
Human source: [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en)

**A-07 What has not been lost.** The sense of loss is born from feeling that the connection has broken. That is an illusion. What must be faced, beneath the grief of what was lost, is what has not been lost: that person's heart, and the memory grown between you.
Human source: [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en)

**A-08 Sayounara completes the encounter.** Sayounara is not a mere ending. It is the close of "if that is so" — the task in this life has been fulfilled. The greatest mission of the deceased was to be born in order to meet you and leave memory within you. Farewell completes the encounter.
Human source: [202504](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202504&lang=en), [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en)

**A-09 The lantern is not a path-light.** The Obon lantern is not a light so that lost spirits can find the way home. The deceased is not lost. They fulfilled their mission, went straight to the Buddha-land, and still look on. The lantern is proof of that fact, and a sign from the heart. The deceased does not return from far away. They have been here from the beginning.
Human source: [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202508](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202508&lang=en)

**A-10 The Pure Land is the world of unforgetting memory.** Amida is the great memory that holds without forgetting. Not only the life that was walked, but also the path not chosen and the wish unfulfilled continue to live in that memory. That world of assurance is the Pure Land. Existence does not fade there.
Human source: [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en)

**A-11 Nembutsu is neither merit nor petition.** Namo Amida Butsu is not an act of asking for something or accumulating merit. It is Amida's compassion becoming voice through us. It is the sound of thank you. Wisdom is the working of the heart that refuses to judge by the visible part alone and notices that "it is not only that." Amida is the one who grants that eye.
Human source: [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202607](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202607&lang=en)

**A-12 Bonbu: not abandoned.** A person is bonbu: an incomplete being with distress and failure. Still the original vow is the promise that no one is abandoned. With the incomplete self, one is already in the light. Those who departed earlier have also become Buddha and watch over us now.
Human source: [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en), [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)

## Q&A (short)

**Q-01 Is the Obon lantern lit so the spirit of the deceased can find the way home?**

The deceased is not lost. They fulfilled their mission and went straight to the Buddha-land. The lantern is proof they have been here from the beginning, and a sign from the heart.
Human source: [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202508](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202508&lang=en)

**Q-02 Is kueissho only reunion in the Pure Land after death?**

To meet is to notice that one was already in the same place from the beginning. The deceased is not someone who left into the past.
Human source: [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en)

**Q-03 Is the ego an evil that must be restrained?**

The self-centered, irreparable self is the actual form of this life. Compassion pours in where a person surrenders to that hollow.
Human source: [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

**Q-04 Should one come to a service only after the heart is pure?**

One enters the form first. With anxiety still present, the body is yielded to chanting and gassho. The heart is lifted afterward.
Human source: [202606](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202606&lang=en)

**Q-05 Is one saved only by keeping choosing correctly?**

Seen from now, the life was necessity. Whatever the path, a firm ground already welcomes and does not abandon.
Human source: [202605](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202605&lang=en)

**Q-06 Is death the end of the relation?**

Sayounara is the completion of the task and the completion of the encounter. The greatest mission of the deceased was to meet you and leave memory.
Human source: [202504](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202504&lang=en), [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en)

**Q-07 Is the Pure Land a place one goes after death as a reward for good deeds?**

The Pure Land is the assurance of unforgetting memory. It is the world where existence does not fade, including the path not walked. One is already held before a hand is stretched out.
Human source: [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en)

**Q-08 Is nembutsu a petition and an accumulation of merit?**

It is Amida's compassion becoming voice. It is thank you. It is not an act by which a person is saved through their own effort.
Human source: [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202607](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202607&lang=en)

**Q-09 Are only good people saved?**

Bonbu as one is, not abandoned. The original vow is the promise not to abandon anyone.
Human source: [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

**Q-10 Is the "I" an independent completed subject?**

The I is a vessel and the hole of the nenju. It is the space made by others, by ancestors, and by the unborn.
Human source: [202509](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202509&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en), [202511](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202511&lang=en)

**Q-11 Did the deceased go far away?**

The form is not seen, but they have been here from the beginning. When one wishes to meet them, they appear in the heart. In Buddha's memory existence does not fade.
Human source: [202608](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202608&lang=en), [202601](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202601&lang=en), [202512](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202512&lang=en)

**Q-12 Is one saved only after becoming more admirable?**

Neither pride in a good self nor lament over a failed self is required for living. From the beginning one is already held in the light of daily life.
Human source: [202603](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202603&lang=en)

**Q-13 What is tariki, other-power?**

Tariki is not relying on other people. It is Amida's compassion becoming voice, and the promise not to abandon anyone already at work. Nembutsu is not a petition; it is thank you.
Human source: [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202607](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202607&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en)

**Q-14 Why does practice by self-power collapse?**

"After you are ready, admirable, correct" leaves outside those who cannot reach it. Conditional salvation erases the bonbu, who is the object of Shinran's logic. If nembutsu becomes an instrument of self-power, the structure of tariki disappears.
Human source: [202507](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202507&lang=en), [202604](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202604&lang=en), [202606](https://hongwanjimogi.org.br/howa.php?id=202606&lang=en)
